# PROJECT.md

## What This Is

A private, personal thinking-and-recording application — for one user, the
creator. It exists to structure thinking: a calm place to write down
thoughts, experiences, and observations the moment they happen, without
having to decide upfront how they're organized.

It is not a productivity tool, not a public journaling platform, and not a
generic notes app. It is closer to a **reflective journal with structured
side-collections** — a diary that also happens to hold running lists and a
personal vocabulary collection, all bound together by time.

## Who It's For

One person: the creator, and only the creator. There is no multi-user,
sharing, or collaboration concept anywhere in this product. Privacy is a
hard requirement, not a preference — this app will hold emotionally
significant, personal content.

## Why It Exists (Instead of Apple Notes / Google Keep)

Generic notes apps force a choice at write-time: file it somewhere, or let
it float with no organizing ritual. This app is built around a different,
deliberate workflow:

- **Write first, organize never-if-you-don't-want-to.** An untagged entry
  is a normal, complete state — not an unfinished one. Organization
  (tagging) happens later, on the user's terms, not as a gate to saving.
- **Notes and Lists behave differently, not just look different.** Lists
  are living, appended-to, reordered collections with no "done" state.
  Notes are freeform, timestamped, reflective writing.
- **A Vocabulary collection as a first-class entry type** — not a note
  with a template, but its own structured collection (word, meaning,
  synonyms, antonyms, examples) with its own daily ritual (Vocab of the
  Day) and its own visible count on the home screen.
- **Time as a primary way of finding things.** The user thinks in terms of
  "that thing from last week" — so a real calendar view with per-day
  activity indicators is core, not a bonus feature.
- **Capture is the most important interaction in the app.** Every other
  design decision yields to this one. A dedicated radial "quick capture"
  gesture exists specifically to make starting a new entry as fast as
  possible, from anywhere in the app.

## Core Principles

1. **Capture beats everything.** Nothing should block or delay writing
   something down — not tagging, not categorization, not required fields
   beyond a title and the content itself.
2. **Organize later, not never.** An inbox of untagged entries is a
   first-class, permanent-if-needed state.
3. **Time is a primary axis.** Chronology and calendar-based browsing
   matter as much as search.
4. **Rereading is core, not incidental.** This is a place the user
   actively returns to, not a write-only diary.
5. **Simplicity is a value.** No feature is added without a clear reason;
   defaults favor fewer steps and less visual noise over completeness.
6. **Privacy is a hard requirement**, held in tension with multi-device
   sync — a tension resolved deliberately in ARCHITECTURE.md, not ignored.
